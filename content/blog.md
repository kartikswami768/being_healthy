This is where the writing on Notes on Being Human lives: questions, essays, case stories, and shorter observations about health and being human.

The archive is organised by the kind of writing rather than by subject. Topics can overlap freely, and useful connections between pieces are made through links.

- [[blog/questions|Everyday Questions]] — practical questions about health, the body, food, exercise, habits, and ordinary experiences.
- [[blog/essays|Essays]] — longer explorations of health, medicine, psychology, behaviour, and ideas that need more room.
- [[blog/cases|Case Stories]] — medical cases and the reasoning that makes them interesting or instructive.
- [[blog/notes|Notes & Observations]] — shorter thoughts, observations, and pieces of learning.

As the archive grows, these sections remain the entry points; the links between individual pieces are what make the collection a network rather than four separate lists.
