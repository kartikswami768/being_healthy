## I found my groove

So college ended in mid-May. Ended with a bang, and with a lot of stress around dealing with the people around me. Yes, manage. Because there were a lot of expectations from all sides—friends, family, relatives, seniors. And some of those “close friends” made the situation a little bit more difficult, because college was ending and they were feeling a lot of things. They were overwhelmed.

On the other hand, I was happy because I would be free in a few days to do whatever I wanted. No internship duties and no upcoming exams to prepare for. Yes, there was this NEET PG coming up, and people around me were beginning to feel the fight getting closer to them. But I had already decided that I would enter that race only to walk.

So yeah, people were sad and overwhelmed, and I was trying to feel free. And that kind of behaviour hurts people, when you are so nonchalant about such a major event in your life.

Wrapping up all those things took about 10 days. Then the campaign started. The campaign to get the internship logbook signed and complete all the formalities.

All that kept going on. Went on a trek in the first 10 days with friends. Went to relatives. Did countless trips from home to college and back again for that campaign.

I was finally able to get some time to myself after mid-June. Three days I was alone at home because everyone was out of town. Best period I could have asked for.

I learned cooking. Discovered the tastiest protein shake, which I am still using as my breakfast and sharing the recipe for with friends. Slept a lot. Had time with myself.

Only after that did I have the courage to even think about going to the gym. Otherwise, I was so stressed and my body was so out of rhythm that I couldn’t follow through on anything I had planned. And I could not do anything that needed significant effort.

Joined the gym in late June.

By the first week of July, I was on the road with my sister and mother on a trip through Rajasthan. It was about a six-day trip. Driving on open highways with complete freedom. Such a dream come true.

Came back to the gym by mid-July. I used to go to the gym in the evening, near sunset or even after sunset. Used to wake up late in the morning, almost near noon.

The biggest stressor in my life was my bad sleep cycle, and this kind of pattern was not helping. I was not feeling good. I was mostly free all day but had no motivation to do anything. I was not feeling fresh even after waking up.

But the thing is, consistency matters.

I knew the problem. I had already taken two steps towards fixing it. One, gym. And the second, diet.

I was on a strict diet now. My weight had gone past 91 kg. Most of it was around my belly, which is popularly known as central fat. And as many people know, it is the worst kind of fat that anyone can store in their body.

But these two steps were directly targeting the problem. And my effort to reset my sleep cycle continued.

I took melatonin. Since I could not handle big doses because they would make me feel tired and drowsy the next day, I would often take 1.5 mg. It was good enough to push me to sleep, but it was not enough to break the habits that were actually keeping me up.

Turns out, when you wake up—and more importantly, when you see the sun—has a lot to do with when you are going to sleep and when you are going to wake up.

My room went through some changes. We fitted a window AC in my room, and for that we had to remove the wooden door panels of my window. They were replaced by translucent plastic sheets.

And more importantly, this window faced east.

So when the sun rises, I would be directly facing the sun. In a way, my room finally got the sunlight that I needed for waking up and for sleeping that night.

Slowly and slowly, I got off melatonin. Started dimming my room lights around sunset. Started using bright light during the day. Started getting morning sunlight.

And now I am not able to stay awake past 11.

I still have trouble sleeping sometimes. But I almost never have issues waking up now.

I wake up without an alarm by 6:30 in the morning. I mostly feel fresh. Even if I’m sleepy because I slept late, I have a lot of energy.

This energy is like an itch.

And this itch won’t go away until I do something productive.

This itch for productivity is getting so strong these days that staying productive is not enough anymore. It needs something more. I have got no clue what it needs.

But my weight is near 83 kg now. People are complimenting me on how I look. And I feel so much potential and energy to do things that I am now a little bit stressed that I am not doing enough.

Maybe that last thought has always been there.

Maybe.

But now I have found my groove, and now I am doing something. And not just something. I am doing a lot.

And if I remain consistent, I am going to go places.

> By the way, I was able to realize all this because of that special friend. So, thanks to them.
