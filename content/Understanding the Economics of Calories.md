# Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE)

When we talk about daily calorie burn, we are looking at your **Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE)**[span_0](start_span)[span_0](end_span). Your TDEE is made up of four distinct components[span_1](start_span)[span_1](end_span):

1. **BMR**: Resting energy expenditure[span_2](start_span)[span_2](end_span)
2. **NEAT**: Non-Exercise Activity Thermogenesis[span_3](start_span)[span_3](end_span)
3. **EAT**: Exercise Activity Thermogenesis[span_4](start_span)[span_4](end_span)
4. **TEF**: Thermic Effect of Food[span_5](start_span)[span_5](end_span)

---

## Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR)

This is the amount of energy our body needs to maintain our body structure[span_6](start_span)[span_6](end_span). 

* The bigger the body (along with age, sex, and some other physiological factors), the bigger the energy needed to maintain it[span_7](start_span)[span_7](end_span).
* **The Car Analogy**: Think of it like a car—the bigger and more expensive your car is, the higher its maintenance cost rises[span_8](start_span)[span_8](end_span). 
* **How to increase BMR?** Simply make your car bigger **and** expensive[span_9](start_span)[span_9](end_span).

Our body contains two main dynamic tissues that can grow or shrink considerably during our lifetime[span_10](start_span)[span_10](end_span):
* <mark style="background-color: rgba(76, 175, 80, 0.25); color: inherit; padding: 2px 4px; border-radius: 4px;">Muscle Tissue</mark>: The more metabolically active tissue per kilogram[span_11](start_span)[span_11](end_span).
* <mark style="background-color: rgba(244, 67, 54, 0.25); color: inherit; padding: 2px 4px; border-radius: 4px;">Adipose Tissue</mark>: The less metabolically active tissue per kilogram[span_12](start_span)[span_12](end_span).

Here, the muscle tissue is the **luxury component** of your body[span_13](start_span)[span_13](end_span). It raises the value of parts of your car and thus its maintenance cost[span_14](start_span)[span_14](end_span).

> [!quote] The Terry Jeffords Example
> Have you watched the show *Brooklyn 99*? Terry Jeffords used to eat more than Scully and his partner, yet he was less fat[span_15](start_span)[span_15](end_span). He used to say he needed **10,000 calories** just to maintain his muscle mass[span_16](start_span)[span_16](end_span). If he ate less than that, his muscles would not exist anymore[span_17](start_span)[span_17](end_span).

## Thermic Effect of Food (TEF)

TEF is the amount of energy your body uses to digest, absorb, and process the nutrients in the diet you eat[span_18](start_span)[span_18](end_span). 

The calories you eat don't reach your body to be used as such—some of these calories are spent just moving them from the outside to the inside[span_19](start_span)[span_19](end_span).

Among the major macros, **protein has the highest thermic effect** (almost twice to thrice that of carbohydrates)[span_20](start_span)[span_20](end_span). For carbohydrates, the more complex they are, the higher the thermic effect[span_21](start_span)[span_21](end_span).

### Macronutrient Breakdown

| Macro | Estimated TEF |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Protein** | <span style="color: #4CAF50; font-weight: bold;">20 – 30%</span>[span_22](start_span)[span_22](end_span) |
| **Carbohydrates** | <span style="color: #FF9800; font-weight: bold;">5 – 10%</span>[span_23](start_span)[span_23](end_span) |
| **Fats** | <span style="color: #F44336; font-weight: bold;">0 – 3%</span>[span_24](start_span)[span_24](end_span) |

### Protein Energy Stages

| Energy Stage | Description | Value |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Gross Energy** | The total raw energy inside the food (measured in a laboratory bomb calorimeter)[span_25](start_span)[span_25](end_span). | `~5.65 kcal/g`[span_26](start_span)[span_26](end_span) |
| **Net Available Energy** | The energy absorbed by your body after accounting for undigested food and nitrogen waste in urine (the 4 kcal/g on labels)[span_27](start_span)[span_27](end_span). | `4.0 kcal/g`[span_28](start_span)[span_28](end_span) |
| **Metabolizable Energy** | The actual energy left over for your cells to use after subtracting the energy spent on digestion (TEF)[span_29](start_span)[span_29](end_span). | `~2.8 to 3.2 kcal/g`[span_30](start_span)[span_30](end_span) |

### TEF in Staple Foods

| Staple Food | Estimated TEF |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Steel Cut Oats** | `10 – 12%`[span_31](start_span)[span_31](end_span) |
| **Brown Rice** | `8 – 10%`[span_32](start_span)[span_32](end_span) |
| **Whole Wheat Roti** | `8 – 10%`[span_33](start_span)[span_33](end_span) |
| **White Bread** | `5 – 6%`[span_34](start_span)[span_34](end_span) |
| **White Rice** | `5%`[span_35](start_span)[span_35](end_span) |

> [!tip] Fat Loss Strategy
> If you get most of your calories from **protein** and **complex carbs**, it will be significantly better for your fat loss journey[span_36](start_span)[span_36](end_span).

## Non-Exercise Activity Thermogenesis (NEAT)

> [!important] The Strongest Weapon
> NEAT is the strongest weapon for daily energy expenditure, yet it is almost always ignored[span_37](start_span)[span_37](end_span).

Let's look at the weekly time breakdown[span_38](start_span)[span_38](end_span):

<div style="background-color: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05); padding: 15px; border-radius: 8px; border-left: 4px solid #2196F3; margin-bottom: 10px;">

* **Total Hours in a Week**: `168 Hours`[span_39](start_span)[span_39](end_span)
* **Sleep (8 hrs/day)**: `-56 Hours` &rarr; `112 Hours Left`[span_40](start_span)[span_40](end_span)
* **Exercise (1 hr/day, 6 days/wk)**: `-6 Hours` &rarr; <span style="color: #2196F3; font-weight: bold;">106 Hours Left</span>[span_41](start_span)[span_41](end_span)

</div>

How you spend those **106 hours** matters immensely[span_42](start_span)[span_42](end_span). You could stay sedentary, or you can actively optimize NEAT[span_43](start_span)[span_43](end_span):

* Walk frequently[span_44](start_span)[span_44](end_span)
* Stand more[span_45](start_span)[span_45](end_span)
* Take the stairs[span_46](start_span)[span_46](end_span)
* Walk after meals[span_47](start_span)[span_47](end_span)
* Commute actively[span_48](start_span)[span_48](end_span)

Because NEAT accumulates over many hours throughout the day, the total energy spent can become substantial[span_49](start_span)[span_49](end_span).
